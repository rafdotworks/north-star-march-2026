# Personal README

I'm a Design Engineer focused on clarity, craft, and calm collaboration. This document helps you understand how I work best and how we can work together effectively.

## Who I Am

I work on AI-driven products where design, product, and engineering meet. I focus on making sense of complex systems and care about taste, restraint, and shipping work that holds up over time.

I value presence, calm joy, and sustainable pace. If I seem quieter, I'm usually thinking, not disengaged. Clarity and kindness go together.

## How I Work

### Communication

I communicate with directness and calm. I prioritize context first, decisions over endless options, and prefer to name issues early rather than let them linger. My style is thoughtful and designed to move things forward.

- **Direct and calm** - no hidden agendas or passive messaging
- **Context first** - I share the full picture before diving into details
- **Decisions over options** - I narrow choices rather than create analysis paralysis
- **Early transparency** - if something feels off, I prefer to name it early

### What Energizes Me

I do my best work under these conditions:

- **Clear priorities** - knowing what matters most
- **Fewer parallel threads** - depth over breadth
- **Time to think** - room to process before reacting
- **Trust to execute** - autonomy within aligned goals
- **One clear problem at a time** - focused scope
- **High trust, low ceremony** - minimal process, maximum alignment
- **Shared ownership** - true collaboration, not delegation
- **Respect for focus** - protection for deep work

### What Challenges Me

I do my best work when these challenges are minimized:

- **Ambiguous ownership** - unclear who owns what
- **Constant interruptions** - fragmented attention
- **Too many competing priorities** - everything urgent means nothing is
- **Optimizing for optics** - style over substance

## Availability & Rhythm

I work best in focused blocks. Some days go deep, some days stay lighter. Output matters more than clock-watching. I default to async communication and sync when it truly matters.

## Working With Me

Here's how to get the best out of our collaboration:

- **Share the real problem** - not just symptoms, but the underlying challenge
- **Tell me what matters most** - explicit priorities help me focus
- **Give me room to simplify** - I'll look for the clearest path forward
- **Trust the process** - I value momentum, but not chaos
- **Be direct with feedback** - honest input helps us both improve
- **Protect focus time** - I'll be more responsive when I have uninterrupted time to think
