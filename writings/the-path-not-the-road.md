# The Path, not the Road

Change isn't a road with a clear end.

It's a path; this summer reminded me that the point isn't reaching a fixed place, but staying open, real, and growing along the way.

At Coinbase, I stepped into a new environment. Different from what I was used to and learned what it means to design at scale, in the middle of strong politics and ambitious goals. I deeply believed in the mission of giving developers more leverage, and that conviction stayed with me.

I also wrapped my time at Voiceflow earlier. Now I'm established in Toronto for another year, focused on building long-term with people I believe in.

I'm into something extremely exciting with the Berachain folks (more to come).

Change feels less like disruption, more like rhythm. The anchors I hold onto: authenticity, openness, growth.
