# Working Philosophy

## 1. Build what is real

I avoid the pretty option. I build what actually moves people forward. The work has to hold up in reality, not just in Figma.

## 2. Progress over movement

I care about momentum with direction. Clarity first, output second, aesthetics third.

## 3. Narrative before execution

Every project starts by answering one question: Why does this matter? The story guides the design, the engineering, and the energy.

## 4. Design and engineering as one craft

I work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineers. Same level, same ownership, same obligation. We build by thinking together, not throwing work over a fence.

## 5. Intuition-led, user-shaped

I listen deeply, but I trust intuition first. Users give signals. I translate those into decisions that make sense for both the human and the business.

## 6. Build for people, not metrics

Metrics tell you when you're right. People tell you why. I design for the human first.

## 7. Launch early, refine always

Share. Build. Deliver. Iterate. Repetition over perfection. Velocity with intention.

## 8. Clarity is momentum

When I'm unsure, I choose the path that creates the clearest next step. Clarity accelerates everything.
