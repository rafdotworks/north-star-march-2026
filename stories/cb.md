Context

Coinbase's Developer Platform exists to make building onchain products reliable, safe, and scalable.

By the time I joined, the infrastructure was already powerful.
What teams struggled with was the first interaction.

Developers could technically do everything they needed.
They just didn't feel confident doing it.

---

The Problem

Early developer touchpoints were slow and unintuitive.
- Time to first query was long.
- Cost and execution time were unclear.
- Infrastructure setup felt heavy before any value was visible.

This created friction at the exact moment trust needed to be established.

If early interactions feel opaque, developers hesitate.
And hesitation is expensive in financial systems.

---

The Tension

Coinbase carries a strong brand promise around reliability and safety.

The challenge was shaping new product surfaces that:
- Felt trustworthy from the first interaction.
- Did not over-abstract critical financial behavior.
- Balanced power with clarity.

We needed to reduce friction without hiding what mattered.

---

My Role

I worked as a senior product AI designer embedded in the Developer Platform team.

My focus was on:
- SQL Playground.
- Embedded Wallets.
- Early exploration of Pay with USDC flows.

These were the surfaces where developers and merchants first formed trust in the platform.

---

Key Decisions

1. Make time and cost legible
Rather than optimizing only for speed, I focused on helping users understand what was happening when they ran a query.
Predictability mattered more than raw performance.

2. Lower the barrier before infrastructure commitment
The SQL Playground became a way to explore data without committing to full setup.
This let developers learn safely before integrating deeply.

3. Preserve reliability over novelty
Given the Coinbase brand, we avoided over-stylized or experimental UI patterns.
The product needed to feel stable, boring in the right ways, and dependable.

---

Outcome

While I can't share specific metrics, the work helped shape:
- Clearer first-contact experiences.
- More predictable developer workflows.
- Internal alignment around how power and safety should surface in product UI.

Progress here was incremental by design.
In financial infrastructure, confidence compounds slowly.

---

Reflection

This work reinforced a principle I carry into all platform design:

Trust isn't communicated through features.
It's communicated through predictability, transparency, and restraint.

Especially when money is involved.
